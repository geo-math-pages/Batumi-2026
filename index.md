# Workshop: Formal Groups in Cobordisms and Related Structures
### Batumi, Georgia | September 2026

---

## Overview
The workshop focuses on the interplay between formal group laws, cobordism theory, and their applications in algebraic topology. 

### Key Topics:
* Complex Cobordism and Formal Group Laws
* Equivariant K-theory
* Topological Data Analysis (TDA)

One of the central objects of discussion will be the structure of the complex cobordism ring
$$ \Omega_*^{MU} \cong \mathbb{Z}[x_1, x_2, \dots], \quad \deg x_i = 2i $$ and related cobordism theories.
